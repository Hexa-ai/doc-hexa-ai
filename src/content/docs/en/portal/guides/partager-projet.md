---
title: Give a subcontractor temporary access
description: "Share a project with someone outside the company, with an end date."
sidebar:
  order: 3
---

**Role needed**: Administrator.

1. Open the project (**Projets**), then **↗ Partager**.
2. Enter the subcontractor's e-mail.
3. Choose **Lecteur** to view, **Opérateur** if they must also acknowledge alarms, **Gestionnaire** if they must edit.
4. Fill in the **end of sharing**: access stops by itself on that date.
5. **Partager**. They appear under **Partages en cours**.

To cut access before the date: **Retirer**. They see only this project, never the rest of your company. See [Sharing with an outside person](/en/portal/administration/external-sharing/).
