---

1. Sign in: if you have only one project, it opens directly.
2. **Avancement** tab: the current stage and the news (direct links to new questions and document versions).
3. **Questions** tab ▸ **À répondre** filter: open a question, write in **Votre réponse**, **Envoyer ma réponse**.
4. **Documents** tab ▸ **Analyse fonctionnelle**: read the latest version of the document.
5. Click **Projet suivi** to receive at most one summary e-mail a day.

See [Following an integration project](/en/portal/projects/integration/).
