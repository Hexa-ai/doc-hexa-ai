title: Set up a wall monitoring screen
description: "Show a dashboard permanently on a screen, with continuous refresh and automatic opening at sign-in."

  order: 6
---

**Role needed**: Manager or Administrator, to set the dashboard.

1. Open the dashboard to display and click **Modifier**.
2. Tick **affichage permanent (écran mural)**: values update about every 10 seconds, with no time limit. **Enregistrer**.
3. On the screen's computer, sign in with a **Lecteur** account, then **Mon compte ▸ Page d'accueil ▸ a specific dashboard**, pick this dashboard and **Enregistrer**.
4. At each sign-in the dashboard opens by itself. On the **Carte**, the **Mode kiosque** button steps through the markers every 12 seconds.

See [Live, weather and commands](/en/portal/dashboards/commands/) and [Finding your way around](/en/portal/getting-started/navigation/).
