---
title: Print a diagram dossier
description: "Set the company title block then export a dossier of sheets as one PDF."
sidebar:
  order: 7
---

1. **Administration ▸ Mon entreprise ▸ Plans et impression**: choose the title block style, company, author and text sizes. **Enregistrer**.
2. In the project's **Schémas** or **Synoptiques** tab, right-click the folder ▸ **Réglages du plan**: project title, sheet prefix, revision index and date.
3. Check a sheet's **Mise en page** (editor ▸ **…** ▸ **Mise en page**).
4. Folder menu ▸ **Imprimer le dossier…**: choose cover page, table of contents, bill of materials, terminal-strip plan, then start the export.

Without the [Designer option](/en/portal/synoptics/designer/), the PDF carries the "my.hexa-ai.fr" watermark. Details: [Layout, printing and export](/en/portal/synoptics/export/).
