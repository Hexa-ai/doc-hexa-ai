**Goal**: find on one page a site's boxes, their alarms, a dashboard and their documents. **Role needed**: Manager or Administrator.

1. In the menu, open **Projets**, then **+ Nouveau projet**.
2. Give a **name** (the site or machine) and, if you wish, a description.
3. Tick the **boxes to attach** then **Créer le projet**. The project page opens.
4. **Équipements** tab: your boxes, their status and last contact. **Alarmes** tab: what is ongoing.
5. **Tableaux de bord** tab ▸ **Ouvrir le tableau du projet**: it shows each box's status. **Modifier** then **+ Bloc** to add curves, gauges or weather ([build a dashboard](/en/portal/dashboards/dashboards/#build-a-dashboard)).
6. **Documents** tab ▸ **Ajouter des fichiers**: drop the plan or manual, or drag files onto a folder.

Going further: [Projects](/en/portal/projects/projects/), [Documents](/en/portal/projects/documents/).
