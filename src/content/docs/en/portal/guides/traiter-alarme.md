---
title: Get notified and handle an alarm
description: "Receive alarms on your phone, see what is happening and understand who is notified."
sidebar:
  order: 5
---

1. **On your phone**: install the app ([Phone app](/en/portal/getting-started/mobile/)) then, on the **Alarmes** page, **Activer** (enable) notifications on this device.
2. **See what is happening**: the **à traiter** counter in the header opens the alarms. **Fil de l'eau** (live feed) shows ongoing alarms; a finished alarm from today appears there marked **provisoire** until the daily report arrives.
3. **Understand**: click the box or open its project's **Alarmes** tab; **Signalements** give what technicians reported on site (photos included).
4. **Set who is notified**: **Administration ▸ Destinataires ▸ Alertes**: for each box, the people, channels (mail, SMS, call) and escalation ([Alarm recipients](/en/portal/alarms/alarm-recipients/)).

An **Opérateur** (or above) can acknowledge alarms; a **Lecteur** only views them.
