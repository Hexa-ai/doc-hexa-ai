---
title: Invite a colleague with the right rights
description: "Create a colleague's access by choosing their role and scope."
sidebar:
  order: 2
---

**Role needed**: Administrator.

1. Menu **Administration ▸ Destinataires**, **Accès portail** tab, then **+ Ajouter un utilisateur**.
2. Enter their name and e-mail.
3. Choose the **role** that fits what they must do:
   - only follow: **Lecteur** (Viewer);
   - acknowledge alarms and control: **Opérateur** (Operator);
   - edit dashboards, synoptics, documents and alarm subscriptions within their scope: **Gestionnaire** (Manager);
   - manage everything: **Administrateur**.
4. Choose the **scope**: whole company, some **projects**, or some **boxes**.
5. **Créer + envoyer l'invitation**. They receive an e-mail to choose their password.

You can later change their role or scope from the list (**Éditer**). Rights in detail: [Roles and scopes](/en/portal/administration/roles/).
