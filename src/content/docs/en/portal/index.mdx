**myHexa** is the online portal that brings all your HAI-P200 boxes together, at [my.hexa-ai.fr](https://my.hexa-ai.fr). Each box sends its measurements and alarms there: you monitor the whole fleet in one place, without connecting to the boxes one by one.

This part of the documentation describes the portal. For installing and configuring a box (network, acquisition, embedded Data-Explorer), see the [HAI-P200 controller](/en/) documentation.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Monitor the fleet" icon="laptop">
    The list of your boxes and their live status, the map, each box's page and the history of its variables.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Dashboards" icon="seti:pipeline">
    Dashboards per box or per project, which you build yourself, with PDF reports sent automatically.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Alarms" icon="warning">
    Current alarms and their history, phone notifications, and the recipients the boxes notify.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Field" icon="setting">
    Technicians report their interventions by scanning the box's QR code, with photos.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
