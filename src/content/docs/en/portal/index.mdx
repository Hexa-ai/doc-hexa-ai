**myHexa** is Hexa-AI's online portal, at [my.hexa-ai.fr](https://my.hexa-ai.fr). Your HAI-P200 boxes send their measures and alarms there: you follow your installations from a browser or a phone, without connecting to each box. The portal interface is in French: labels are given in French in bold, with an English gloss on first use.

To install and configure a box (network, acquisition, embedded Data-Explorer), see the [HAI-P200 controller](/en/) documentation.
